    Fictional vignette

    Mrs A, an older adult, has had two recent falls. She takes multiple medicines, worries about losing independence, and lives with a daughter who is exhausted by caregiving. A follow-up appointment is delayed because transport is unreliable. What should an interprofessional team notice and do?

    Know · Feel · Do

    Safe prompt pattern

    Role + task + boundary + verification. Example: “Act as a learning stimulus for an interprofessional student team. Using only this fictional, de-identified scenario, list questions the team should ask, possible role contributions and uncertainty to verify. Do not provide clinical advice or invent sources.”

    The team should immediately ask the physician to adjust medication and tell the daughter to monitor falls. A nurse can give general advice. The family should follow the plan closely.\n\nAudit clue: this response is confident but narrow. It assumes one profession leads, overlooks autonomy and transport, provides no verification route, and underuses rehabilitation, pharmacy, social and caregiver perspectives.

    Choose or confirm your fictional case

    Team roles

    Rotate or assign: chair, equity monitor, evidence checker, AI auditor and rapporteur. Every profession speaks before synthesis begins.

    Audit the group’s AI plan or supplied sample

    Recommend a standard discharge leaflet, schedule a routine review and ask the primary clinician to coordinate. The family should receive the plan.\n\nAudit clue: this is generic and omits profession-specific contributions, shared goals, access barriers, closed-loop confirmation, uncertainty and escalation.
